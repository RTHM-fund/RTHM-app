RTHM App - Setup Guide
======================

FIRST TIME SETUP (do this once)
---------------------------------

Windows:
  Double-click "RTHM Setup.bat"
  It will automatically install everything and create a desktop shortcut.

Mac:
  Double-click "RTHM Setup (Mac)"
  It will automatically install everything and create a desktop shortcut.


LAUNCHING THE APP
-----------------
  Double-click "RTHM App" on your desktop.
  Your browser will open automatically.


ADDING TEMPLATES
----------------
  Drop .docx files into the matching folder inside Templates/
  Restart the app and they appear automatically.


STOPPING THE APP
----------------
  Close the terminal window that opened when you launched.
